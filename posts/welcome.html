hi, welcome in

Okay so, I made a website. A tiny, slightly chaotic, very sunflower-y website. If you're reading this, hiii, you found it, and I'm a little shy about it already.

For the longest time all my favourite things were scattered everywhere: films in a notes app, half-written poems in the back of class notebooks, shers screenshotted at 2am and never seen again. This is me finally putting them all on one shelf.

कुछ बातें लिखने के लिए होती हैं, कहने के लिए नहीं।
(some things are meant to be written, not said.)

So here's the plan, loosely: I'll write about the films and books that rearrange something in me, put up poems when I'm brave enough, and sometimes just ramble about winter and chai. No schedule, no pressure. Just a corner.

Pull up a chair. Stay as long as you like.

love, riya ✿
